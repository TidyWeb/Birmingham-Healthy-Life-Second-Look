Chapter S3

Results at a glance

Signal Box raised concerns about interpretation, certainty and accountability, while all four legal triggers had no concern raised.

In short

My first pass raised 17 concerns, and 10 questions with no concern raised. All four legal-trigger questions had no concern raised. These are issues for me to investigate, not findings of harm.

Three findings to carry forward

  1. In Unit 2 I framed ethics mainly as privacy. Signal Box suggests privacy is the part my project largely gets right: all four legal triggers had no concern raised. The wider concerns are about group harm, false certainty and accountability to the people described.
  2. The main risk is presentation claiming more than the evidence. Firm rankings where the estimates overlap, and measures shown side by side that could be taken as causes. The fix is accuracy, not softening: show uncertainty where it matters and keep to association, not cause.
  3. I found two good Unit 2 decisions that are claimed but not tested. The model leaves ethnicity and deprivation out of its features, but I have not checked proxy overlap or whether model errors differ between communities. Until I test them, these are intentions rather than evidence. Notebook 07 has since tested both; see Proxies, fairness and crime.
