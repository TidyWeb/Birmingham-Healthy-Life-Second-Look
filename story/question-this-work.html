Question this work

The work is mine. You can ask me to explain or check a choice; if I find an error, I correct and record it.

In short

Questions are welcome. Readers can raise an issue about a page or result. I review the evidence, make any correction, and log what changed and why.

How to ask

Use the Question this work form on GitHub. Tell me which page or area you mean and what you are questioning. Evidence or reasoning is useful but optional. Please keep the conversation on the issue; do not include personal information. This form needs a free GitHub account. That's a limit of this route.

This is my work. Questions are welcome, and I make and log corrections when an error is shown. Readers can question it; they do not edit the analysis or site.

What happens next

I reply within 10 working days. I read the question and check it against the source, notebook or wording involved. If I confirm an error, I correct it and add a dated entry below. If the evidence does not show an error, I explain what I checked and why I have left the result as it is. A question does not automatically mean the published result is wrong.
