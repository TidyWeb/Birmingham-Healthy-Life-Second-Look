Chapter S9

Stage 6: Hotspots

The map can show where measured differences cluster. It cannot say what caused them.

In short

I grouped repeated large gaps between neighbouring areas into hotspot patches. The screen's concern is that residual and hotspot maps can look like proof, so I describe estimates and spatial association plainly.

What I did in Unit 2

Notebook 06Large neighbour gaps grouped into connected patches.

I joined large-gap pairs where they shared an area, then mapped the largest patches. I also compared each area's measured HLE with the model estimate to see where the model's residuals clustered.

What the screen asked

Q14 — Could an output be treated as proof rather than a prompt? Concern. The screen found that a residual map could read as an “underperforming” place, even though a residual is only the difference between observed HLE and the model's estimate.

What I found

The hotspot pattern shows where large area-level differences cluster. The residual map shows what the selected model estimates fail to account for. Neither map identifies a cause or describes each resident.

What changes

I changed only the residual-map and hotspot-map captions. They now name the model estimate and the spatial association, and state that the maps do not show cause. The size of a measured difference remains visible.
