Chapter S10

Stage 7: Dashboards and site

I made an area-level story, maps and dashboards. I need to be as clear about their limits as I am about their findings.

In short

I published named area results through a story site and dashboards. The screen found risks in certainty, purpose, challenge and evaluation; the answer is to state the limits and make the claims match the evidence.

What I did in Unit 2

I built a public story site, a Tableau dashboard and a Leaflet dashboard with area-level maps, values and comparisons. The project describes areas; it does not make an automated decision about a person.

What the screen asked

Q1 — Could the work shape a decision that matters to someone? Concern. No individual decision is made, but named maps are public and can inform how professionals and others compare places.

Q3 — Is the purpose vague or likely to grow? Concern. The project question is clear, but Unit 2 did not state excluded uses.

Q4 — Does the work mainly benefit me while others bear a cost? Concern. The screen recorded that I gain assessment and portfolio evidence while the project names places without involving local readers. The accuracy response is to show uncertainty and avoid claims beyond the data.

Q14 — Could an output be treated as proof rather than a prompt? Concern. Exact values and ranks can appear firmer than the estimate intervals allow.

Q17 — Can someone understand, question or challenge the description? Concern. Unit 2 had no public route to question how an area was described.

Q18 — Were affected people left out of design and testing? Concern. No residents, community groups or local professionals tested the dashboards.

Q20 — Could a failure lead to harm? Concern. The screen found no physical risk. Its accuracy concern is that a map could overstate what area estimates show.

Q21 — Was the work tested only by its creator? Concern. I checked the dashboards myself, not with real users.

Q22 — Is there a tested way to pause, override or fall back? Concern. The static site can be taken down, but Unit 2 had no written correction, takedown or archive procedure.

Q23 — Could the work be used outside its purpose? Concern. Public combinations of health, crime, ethnicity and food-environment measures need explicit purpose limits and caveats so association is not presented as explanation.

Cleared questions. Q12: no consequential decisions are automated. Q13: there is no reviewer workflow. Q16: AI use is disclosed. Q19: the project does not change jobs or monitoring. Q24: I am named as owner, although a review cadence is not recorded. Q26: the UK project is not deployed in the EU or in a listed high-risk area. Q27: the work is for Birmingham and should not be generalised to other places. These are no flags, not a claim that every part of the project is safe.

What I found

The screen found a clear gap between publishing a dashboard and having evidence that people can interpret, question or correct it. I have not treated a creator-only check as user testing.

What changes

The site now states the use limits and carries uncertainty and association caveats. A public question route, user testing, and the correction and retirement policy are covered later in this second look.
