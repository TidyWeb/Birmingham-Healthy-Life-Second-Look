What's here

I built Ethical Considerations in AI as an independent companion to the Code Institute LMS lesson for fellow students, then created Signal Box: the AI ethics check within it. I applied Signal Box to my Unit 2 Birmingham Healthy Life project; Unit 3 is that ethical rework and a practical test of whether Signal Box works. Start with Why a second look, or visit the original Unit 2 project site. The six notebooks are available here as read-only pages.
