    Actual data. An exploratory surface.

    Healthy life expectancy (HLE) is the estimated years lived in good health. HLE average here is the mean of male and female estimates, calculated for this project; it is not an ONS statistic. Its approximate 95% range assumes independent errors for men and women and is probably too narrow.

    Measures describe areas and use their own definitions, units, denominators and periods. They are linked with HLE in these data; a map cannot establish that one measure is responsible for another. Household deprivation includes a health and disability dimension that overlaps HLE, so it is context only. Machine-learning predictions are not shown. Rank intervals and tenth-band probabilities come from Notebook 08’s 10,000 simulated draws.

    Ethnicity is shown as six broad groups, because the purpose was to compare broad cultural differences; very small groups add nothing to that and are not shown separately.

    The basemap is OpenStreetMap and needs an internet connection. If you open this page as a local file, the tiles may not load, because the OpenStreetMap tile service needs to know which website is asking.

    Adult smoking: OHID Fingertips, Annual Population Survey (indicator 92443), 2022–24 pooled. County and unitary-authority estimates are available for 11 of these 37 authorities; district and neighbourhood estimates are not supplied here.
